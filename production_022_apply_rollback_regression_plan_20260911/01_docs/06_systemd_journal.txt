systemd / journal (observe only)
================================

Before and after a future APPLY:

  systemctl is-active <expect-ai unit>
  ActiveState=active required

  journalctl -u <expect-ai unit> --since <apply-window>
  count error-level lines
  APPLY must not introduce a new error burst

  Confirm process environ:
    PREDICTION_RUNS_ENABLED is 0 or absent
    EXPECT_AI_ALLOW_MIGRATION_022 is absent after the window

This plan does not contain systemctl start/stop/restart/edit commands
that an agent or wrapper may run. Observation commands belong in a
future Owner-approved execution pack only.
