Owner browser visual steps — public 403 isolation
=================================================

Purpose
-------
Separate a Cloudflare/WAF 403 that hit the APPLY pack's Python
urllib GET from a general-browser outage.

The pack public smoke did not set User-Agent. urllib.request then
sends a Python-urllib default. That can be 403 while a normal
browser is 200.

Do not use PowerShell, curl, Python, or "Copy as cURL" for this
first pass. Use a normal desktop browser only.
Do not log in. Do not POST. Do not open DevTools replay that
changes the request into a scripted client.
Do not re-run OWNER_APPLY.ps1.

SITE_UI_VISUAL_CHECKED starts as NO. Owner records YES only after
these pages are seen in a normal browser.

Browser
-------
Use the Owner everyday browser (Chrome / Edge / Safari / Firefox
as usually used). Do not use a headless or automation profile.

For each URL below:
1. Open a new tab.
2. Paste the URL.
3. Record HTTP-visible outcome: page shows / 403 / other error.
4. If the browser network panel is already open, read status only.
   Do not resend as curl.
5. Do not capture race_id values, horse names, or bundle_json.

URLs (expect-keiba.com only for this first isolation)
-----------------------------------------------------
A. https://expect-keiba.com/
   Expect if general-public OK: HTML 200, site chrome visible.
   Record: BROWSER_ROOT=200 or BROWSER_ROOT=403 or other.

B. https://expect-keiba.com/race.html
   Expect if general-public OK: HTML 200.
   Record: BROWSER_RACE=200 or BROWSER_RACE=403 or other.

C. https://expect-keiba.com/races.html
   Expect if general-public OK: HTML 200.
   Record: BROWSER_RACES=200 or BROWSER_RACES=403 or other.

D. https://expect-keiba.com/api/health
   Expect if general-public OK: JSON 200, ok=true, service=bff.
   Record: BROWSER_API_HEALTH=200 or 403 or other.

E. https://expect-keiba.com/api/predictions
   Research Week known-good: 503 with error.code=OPS_CLOSED.
   Also acceptable: 200 JSON envelope if the week is open.
   Record: BROWSER_API_PREDICTIONS=503_OPS_CLOSED or 200 or 403
   or other. Do not treat a Research Week OPS_CLOSED 503 as a 403.

How to interpret
----------------
All A-D are 200 (and E is 200 or 503 OPS_CLOSED):
  PUBLIC_403_SCOPE=AUDIT_CLIENT_OR_USER_AGENT
  The pack urllib GET was blocked. General browsers work.
  Do not rollback. Do not re-apply 022.

Any of A-D is 403 in the normal browser:
  PUBLIC_403_SCOPE=GENERAL_BROWSER
  Then a read-only origin/WAF diagnosis is needed.
  Still do not rollback, re-apply, or change systemd/env.

pages.dev is not required for this first isolation.
Do not enable POST. Do not switch the site.

SITE_UI_VISUAL_CHECKED=NO until Owner completes A-E.
